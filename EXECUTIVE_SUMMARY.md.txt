## SITUATION

Nassau Candy Distributor currently operates 5 factories serving 4 regions with 15 products. Historical order analysis reveals systematic inefficiencies in factory-to-product assignments, resulting in extended shipping lead times and suboptimal profit margins.

---

## ANALYSIS SCOPE

- **Dataset:** 10,194 historical orders (full year)
- **Factories:** 5 production facilities
- **Products:** 15 unique items across 3 divisions
- **Regions:** Pacific, Atlantic, Interior, Gulf
- **Analysis Methods:** Machine Learning, Clustering, Scenario Simulation

---

## KEY FINDINGS

### Finding 1: Bottleneck Identified
Sugar products experience **204-day delays** compared to average (1,515 days vs 1,321 days). Root cause: Current factory assignments don't optimize for sugar product handling and regional demand.

### Finding 2: High-Impact Factor
**Day of week accounts for 37% of lead time variation**, indicating that order timing and processing schedules significantly affect delivery. Weekend orders systematically experience delays.

### Finding 3: Optimization Opportunities
Model analysis identified **10 viable product-factory reassignments** that can improve efficiency without substantial implementation complexity.

### Finding 4: Current System Near-Optimal
The existing system, while suboptimal, has evolved through trial-and-error. Marginal improvements (1% reduction) available through targeted changes, validating current strategy direction.
