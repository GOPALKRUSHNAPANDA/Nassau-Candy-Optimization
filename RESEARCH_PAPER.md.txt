## ABSTRACT

This research presents a comprehensive data science analysis of Nassau Candy Distributor's shipping operations. Using machine learning and optimization techniques, we analyzed 10,194 orders to identify factory-to-product assignment inefficiencies. Our analysis reveals that sugar product assignments create 204-day delays compared to baseline, and day-of-week significantly impacts lead times (37% importance). We tested 24 optimization scenarios and identified 10 viable reassignments that can improve operational efficiency by 127 days across 1,361 orders with minimal risk. Our findings demonstrate that targeted factory reassignments offer measurable improvements without substantial implementation complexity.

**Keywords:** Supply Chain Optimization, Machine Learning, Clustering, Predictive Analytics, Factory Assignment

## 1. INTRODUCTION

### 1.1 Background
Nassau Candy Distributor operates 5 production facilities across the United States, serving 4 major regions with 15 unique products. The company currently uses static, legacy-based rules to assign products to factories, resulting in suboptimal shipping distances and extended lead times.

### 1.2 Problem Statement
- **Inefficient Factory Assignments:** Current product-factory mappings don't account for regional demand patterns
- **High Lead Times:** Average lead time of 1,321 days with significant variance (900-1,700 days)
- **No Optimization Framework:** No systematic method to evaluate reassignment scenarios
- **Financial Impact:** Suboptimal assignments erode profit margins and customer satisfaction

### 1.3 Research Objective
This project develops a data-driven optimization system that:
1. Predicts shipping outcomes under different factory assignments
2. Identifies high-impact reassignment opportunities
3. Quantifies risks and benefits before implementation
4. Provides actionable recommendations for management

### 1.4 Scope
- 10,194 historical orders analyzed
- 5 production facilities evaluated
- 15 products tested
- 4 geographic regions covered
- 24 optimization scenarios simulated

### 3.3 Feature Engineering

**New Features Created (11):**

1. **Lead_Time_Days** = Ship Date - Order Date
   - Represents actual shipping duration
   - Range: 904-1,642 days

2. **Profit_Margin** = (Gross Profit / Sales) × 100
   - Indicator of product profitability
   - Mean: 65.9%

3. **Order_Month** = Extract month from Order Date
   - Captures seasonal patterns
   - Range: 1-12

4. **Order_DayOfWeek** = Extract day of week from Order Date
   - Tests if day impacts lead time
   - Range: 0-6 (Monday-Sunday)

5. **Units_per_Sale** = Units / (Sales + 0.01)
   - Order composition metric
   - Normalized by sales

6. **Region_Code** = LabelEncoder(Region)
   - Categorical encoding
   - Pacific(0), Atlantic(1), Interior(2), Gulf(3)

7. **ShipMode_Code** = LabelEncoder(ShipMode)
   - Categorical encoding
   - 1st Class(0), 2nd Class(1), Standard(2), Same Day(3)

8. **Division_Code** = LabelEncoder(Division)
   - Categorical encoding
   - Chocolate(0), Other(1), Sugar(2)

**Final Feature Set: 26 features**
- 11 new engineered features
- 15 original numerical/encoded features
